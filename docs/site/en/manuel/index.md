## Who this is for

Anyone who wants to **analyse positions or games** with gammonNet, in a browser or natively. It
assumes no knowledge of the code or of neural networks — only what an equity is in backgammon.

If you are here to decide **whether this engine is any good**, go to the
[scientific documentation](../science/index): it is written to be read by someone who does not
believe us.
